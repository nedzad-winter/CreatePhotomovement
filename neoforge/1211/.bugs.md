# Bug Log

## 2026-02-04: Solar Windmill Bearing SU Doubling Bug

**Severity:** High  
**Status:** ✅ Fixed

### Symptoms
- Solar Windmill Bearing shows 6144 SU instead of correct 3072 SU after world reload
- Value is exactly double the expected amount
- Fresh assemblies worked correctly, but reloading the world caused doubling

### Cause
Create's `KineticNetwork` capacity tracking double-counts capacity:
1. `initFromTE()` sets `unloadedCapacity` from saved NBT
2. `addSilently()` also adds capacity via `calculateAddedStressCapacity()`  
3. Final calculation: `presentCapacity + unloadedCapacity` = 2x expected

### Fix
Override three methods in `SolarWindmillBearingBlockEntity`:
1. `write()` - Zero `capacity` and `lastCapacityProvided` before super.write()
2. `initialize()` - Force `network.initialized = false` to trigger fresh calculation
3. `updateFromNetwork()` - Recalculate correct value and pass to super
